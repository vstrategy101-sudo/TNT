How to post

Six rules for every post.

Post once, publish to both

Schedule in Meta Business Suite (Planner) with both the Instagram account and the Facebook Page ticked. Paste the Instagram caption in the Instagram field and the Facebook caption in the Facebook field.

Use the file sizes listed

Feed and carousels use _4x5.png (1080×1350). Reels and Stories use _9x16 (1080×1920). Don't let the app crop or zoom.

Reels need a cover

Upload the matching end-card JPG from social/covers/ as the cover. Check the grid preview: the profile grid shows the middle 3:4 of it.

Links

Instagram captions say "Link in bio" because caption links don't click. Facebook captions carry the full URL. Stories get a link sticker.

Times are UK time

Feed 12:30 on weekdays, 10:00 on Saturday. Reels 19:00. Story-only posts 18:00. Move a slot by an hour if needed, but keep the order.

Share every post to Story

Same day, a few hours later. For statics use the _9x16.png listed on the post and add a link sticker. For Reels, share the Reel itself.

Before you post · needs CalaFly sign-off

Organic posts follow the same ad rules as paid ones.

These came up when the creatives were made and are still open. UK advertising rules (CAP Code) apply to a brand's own social posts too. Tick them off before the first post goes live.

Ticks are only for your own screen; they aren't saved or shared.

Replies + reporting

Answer people, and track what works.

Comments and DMs
  • Reply within 4 hours on weekdays. First names and plain British English, no hype.
  • "Will it work on my phone?" → unlocked and eSIM-compatible, check calafly.net/compatible-devices.
  • "Do I lose my number?" → no, your own SIM keeps calls, texts and WhatsApp. CalaFly adds data only.
  • "Refund?" → 100% if the eSIM is unused, T&Cs at calafly.net/legal/terms.
  • Never name or criticise another eSIM brand, even if a commenter does.
  • Send order problems to CalaFly support in DM. Don't ask for order details in public comments.
Every Monday, report
  • Followers gained on Instagram and Facebook
  • Per post: reach, saves, shares, link clicks (Stories), profile visits
  • Per Reel: plays, average watch time, % watched to the end
  • Clicks from the link in bio (add UTM tags: ?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=organic_launch)
  • The top three posts by saves and shares. Tell the paid team, so the best ones get budget in the Meta campaigns.
