The five concepts

One reason to choose CalaFly per ad.

G1

Passport. Charger. Data.

Prospecting
Idea
Put data on the packing list next to the things nobody forgets. It makes an eSIM feel like a normal part of getting ready, not a tech purchase.
Why it works
Familiar, scroll-stopping flatlay; no jargon. Good cold opener for people who have never bought an eSIM.
Objects
Passport, sunglasses, charger cable, phone with a checklist.
Person cutout brief

Hands from above dropping the phone into an open suitcase, or a smiling traveller holding passport and phone up to camera. Cut out at the waist, entering from the right.

G2

Same number. Same WhatsApp. No roaming.

Prospecting
Idea
Answer the biggest worry about eSIMs head on: you don't lose your number. Your own SIM keeps calls, texts and WhatsApp; CalaFly only adds data.
Why it works
Two SIM cards side by side explain "data-only eSIM" without the word. The chat with Mum makes it human.
Objects
Physical SIM, CalaFly eSIM card, phone with a family chat.
Person cutout brief

Woman on a sunny café terrace abroad, laughing at her phone mid-message. Cut out at the chest, phone in her right hand, entering from the left.

G3

The roaming bill you won't get.

Prospecting
Idea
Make "pay once, not per day" physical: a receipt that keeps adding £5 a day, stamped out.
Why it works
A concrete total (£70 for two weeks) lands harder than a percentage off, and it names no other brand.
Objects
Till receipt, rubber stamp, phone with order confirmed.
Check
The £5 a day comes from your landing pages (a UK network's daily pass). Small print labels it as an example. Confirm the figure before launch.
Person cutout brief

Man holding up a long receipt with a mock-shocked face, other hand giving a thumbs up with his phone. Cut out at the waist, centre-left.

G4

Trip off? Money back.

Prospecting + retargeting
Idea
Remove the risk of buying early. The refund promise lives on a luggage tag, where travellers already look for reassurance.
Why it works
Pushes people who are still planning to buy now instead of the night before. Strong for site visitors who left.
Objects
Suitcase, luggage tag on string, phone with refund screen.
Person cutout brief

Traveller sitting on an unopened suitcase at home, shrugging and smiling at the phone. Cut out full length, sitting on the suitcase's left edge.

G5

One eSIM app. 200+ countries.

Prospecting
Idea
Show range with a pile of airport tags in CalaFly colours, so any traveller spots their own trip.
Why it works
Gives one ad that fits every destination; pairs well with Advantage+ broad targeting.
Objects
Six luggage tags, phone with destination picker.
Check
calafly.net says 201+ countries; the ad rounds down to 200+.
Person cutout brief

Backpacker mid-stride pointing up at the headline, big grin. Cut out full length, standing in front of the tag pile.

Real people in the ads

The objects are rendered here. Photos of real people need licensed images, which I can't source from this workspace. Two ways to add them:

  • Stock: pick one photo per brief from a licensed library and send them over. Each drops into its ad as a cutout with the same white outline and shadow.
  • Own shoot: one afternoon with two or three people covers all five briefs, and gives Cala faces no competitor has.

Once you pick the concepts, each gets the other three sizes (1:1, 9:16, 1.91:1) and copy for Meta and Google.
