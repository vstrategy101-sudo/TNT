Campaign 1: find new travellers

One campaign, one audience: everyone in the UK. Each destination is its own set of ads, and Meta shows each ad to the people most likely to buy it. At £14 a day this works far better than splitting the money three ways. USA goes live now. Dubai and Turkey join as soon as their prices are confirmed and their creatives are re-rendered with the price.

E

Optimisation routine

Sized for £14 a day. At this budget Meta will show "Learning limited" for weeks; that's expected. Decisions are made on spend thresholds, never on a day or two of data.

Launch day+2 hours
  • Ads Manager → Ads: every ad moves from "In review" to Active. If one is rejected, open it and read the reason (usually a landing page or a word in the text).
  • Events Manager → Overview: PageView and ViewContent are arriving from the ads.
Days 1–3hands off
  • No edits. Every change restarts learning.
Days 1–75 min a day
  • Spend near £14 a day. CPM (cost per 1,000 views) in the £4–9 range is normal for the UK.
  • Link CTR at or above 1%, cost per link click at or below £0.50, landing page views at least 70% of link clicks. If landing page views are far lower, the page is slow on mobile: tell the developer.
Every Thursday20 min
  • Ads: pause an ad that has spent £10 with link CTR under 0.7%, or £20 with no checkout started, or £16 (twice the target cost per purchase) with no purchase. Always keep at least 3 ads live per campaign.
  • Fatigue: if 7-day frequency goes above 2.5 (campaign 1) or 4 (campaign 2), or CTR drops 30% from week 1, add the next creative from the review page: R1 Wheels down, 01 Meet CalaFly, B2 bumper.
  • Countries: once Dubai and Turkey are live, compare cost per purchase by ad. Keep the winners, pause the losers, and brief new creative for the destination that sells.
  • Budget: only change it after 7 days on target. Then +20% every 3–4 days (£14 → £17 → £20).
Day 7 check
  • If campaign 1 has fewer than 5 purchases and cost per purchase is above £16, switch its conversion event to InitiateCheckout for two weeks so Meta has more signals to learn from, then switch back to Purchase.
Milestones
  • 100 purchasers: create a 1% UK lookalike of purchasers and add it as an audience suggestion in campaign 1.
  • Catalogue ready: connect the Merchant Center products as a Meta catalogue and test catalogue ads in campaign 2.
  • Month 2 tests: one creator-style video per destination; USA10 versus no code; 30s brand film for a cold audience.

Check the economics. A USA plan is £7 (£6.30 with USA10). An £8 cost per purchase loses money on the first order, so the account only pays back through repeat trips, bigger plans or other destinations. Ask CalaFly for average order value and repeat rate after month 1, and set the target cost per purchase from those numbers.
