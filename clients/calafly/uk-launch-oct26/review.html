Structure

Campaigns

Google · 70%

  • CF_UK_SEARCH_USA / _TURKEY / _DUBAI: ad groups for eSIM, Data/SIM, Roaming intent and Cities. Phrase + exact match, UK presence targeting. Maximise conversions, then target CPA at about 30 purchases a month.
  • Assets: 15-headline RSA, sitelinks (How it works, Refund policy, Compatible phones, one per country), callouts (the three USPs, Data-only eSIM, Keep your WhatsApp number), 1:1 + 1.91:1 image assets.
  • CF_UK_SEARCH_BRAND: calafly, cala fly, calafly esim, cala esim, calafly.net.
  • CF_UK_DEMANDGEN_PROSPECTING: custom segments from searches like "flights to dubai" and "esim turkey", plus in-market travel audiences. Statics 01–05 + both videos.
  • CF_UK_DEMANDGEN_REMARKETING: site visitors in the last 30 days, split by country page. Statics 06 + 04 + video 02.
  • No Performance Max at launch: it would take spend from searches the Search campaigns already cover. Revisit in month 2 with brand exclusions.

Meta · 30%

  • CF_UK_META_PROSPECTING: Sales objective, Purchase event, campaign budget. Ad sets USA / TURKEY / DUBAI, people living in the UK, 18+, Advantage+ audience with destination and airline interests as suggestions. Exclude purchasers from the last 180 days.
  • Ads: statics 01–05 with 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16 per placement, plus both videos.
  • CF_UK_META_RETARGETING: ad set per country. Visitors to /usa, /turkey or /dubai in the last 30 days, plus 50% video viewers and engagers. Exclude purchasers. Keep frequency under 3 a week.
  • Ads: 06 Still planning, 04 Refund, video 02.

Tracking (week 0, before any spend)

  • Meta Pixel + Conversions API with deduplication. Google Ads purchase conversion with Enhanced Conversions, plus GA4.
  • Consent Mode v2 and a compliant cookie banner. Under UK GDPR and PECR, retargeting relies on consent.
  • Send purchase value and country with every purchase. Add an install event if CalaFly can send one: install rate and refund rate are the quality checks on this account.

Six concepts per country

The look is taken from the CalaFly brand: ink, teal and pink, heavy type, hard offset shadows. Each concept comes in four sizes. The 9:16 versions keep copy clear of the Reels and Stories controls and leave the CTA to Meta's own button.

Each comes in 9:16 (Reels, Stories, Shorts) and 16:9 (YouTube, Demand Gen). They work with the sound off and ship with a silent track; add licensed music when uploading.

UK ad rules

Compliance: built in, plus six sign-offs

Already in every ad

  • No competitors named and no comparisons. No "cheapest", "best", "unlimited", "5G" or "free". Every claim is about CalaFly only (CAP Code 3.33–3.44).
  • Material information on every ad: data-only eSIM, needs an unlocked eSIM-compatible phone (CAP 3.3).
  • The refund claim always carries "Refund if your eSIM is not installed. T&Cs apply, see calafly.net." (CAP 3.9–3.10)
  • "Set up in minutes", not a specific minute count that would need proof.
  • No flags, maps, landmarks, or religious or national symbols. Destinations appear only as airport codes. Nothing political.
  • No countdowns or false urgency. Retargeting never says "we saw you".
  • Small print at least 24px on a 1080px canvas, in high contrast.

CalaFly must confirm before launch

  1. Pay once: no plan for these destinations has a daily charge, auto-renewal or automatic top-up.
  2. Refund terms: what "not installed" means, the time window and how to claim, shown on the landing page and at checkout. A short deadline goes into the ad small print.
  3. Setup time: a typical install time that backs up "minutes".
  4. WhatsApp line: true because the eSIM is data-only and the main SIM stays. The landing page should explain it.
  5. Brand name: the logo reads CALA, the CTA says Calafly. Pick one.
  6. Price: add "from £X" only when it matches the landing page exactly.
Plan

First eight weeks

WEEK 0 · SET UP

Set up

Tracking QA, claim sign-off, landing-page message match per country, refund T&Cs page live.

WEEKS 1–2 · LEARN

Learn

Everything live. No edits for the first 72 hours. The Thursday competitive review starts.

WEEKS 3–4 · CUT

Cut

Pause any ad that has spent twice the target cost per purchase with no purchases. Move Search to target CPA. Rebalance countries.

WEEKS 5–8 · SCALE

Scale

Raise budget 20% every 3–4 days on campaigns under target. Round 2 creative built from what won.

KPIs: cost per purchase (primary), ROAS, landing-page conversion rate, CTR and CPC, 3-second video view rate, install rate, refund rate.

Suggestions

What would lift results most

  1. Show the price once it's confirmed."Pay once" lands much harder with a number next to it. The templates take a price line in minutes.
  2. Match each landing page to its ads.Use the same headline per country, with refund terms and a phone-compatibility checker above the fold, plus Apple Pay and Google Pay.
  3. Send an install reminder about 48 hours before the flight.More installs, fewer refunds, fewer support tickets.
  4. Round 2: creator-style arrival videos.The phone connects as the plane lands, with the winning USP as the hook.
  5. Add reviews as soon as there are any.A new brand asking for card details needs social proof. A rating line slots into the refund and all-USP concepts.
