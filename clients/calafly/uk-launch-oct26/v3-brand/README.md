# CalaFly brand campaign: "Sorted before you fly."

## Brand platform

**Category truth.** Everyone flying from the UK to the USA, Turkey or Dubai needs mobile data. The default options are network roaming add-ons charged by the day, or a scramble at the airport. Most eSIM brands advertise specs and discounts ("unlimited", "5G", "5% off").

**Insight.** UK holidaymakers do their trip admin at home in the week before they fly: online check-in, currency, insurance, the airport parking. Data is the one job left until they land. What they want is for it to be *sorted*.

**Proposition.** CalaFly sorts your mobile data before you leave the UK. You set it up from the sofa in minutes, pay one price for the whole trip, and get a refund if you never install it.

**Campaign line.** *Sorted before you fly.*
**Signature moment.** *Wheels down. Data on.*

| Pillar | What we say | Proof on screen | Never say |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Easy** | Set up in minutes, at home | QR scan from the laptop, "eSIM activated" | "Instant", "one tap", exact minute counts |
| **Fair** | Pay once, not per day | Order screen: one-off payment, no daily charges | Competitor names or "cheapest" |
| **Safe** | Refund if not installed | Account screen: Request refund → sent | "Risk-free", or a refund without "T&Cs apply" |

**Where CalaFly sits against competitors.** Competitors sell specs and discounts. CalaFly sells certainty: a trip with one less thing to think about. It owns the pre-trip admin moment, which no competitor is talking about.

**Tone.** Calm, plain-spoken, British, a little wry. Short sentences. No hype words (unlimited, best, #1, game-changer).

**Identity.** The CALA logo; ink, teal and pink; heavy Inter Display type; pill tags and teal offset cards; real product proof rather than stock lifestyle shots.

**Sonic identity.** Minimal: no music bed. The three-note soft confirm (heard when the eSIM activates) and the warm end-card swell are the brand's sound signature. Use them consistently across every film.

## How to run it

- **Sequence:** reach broad UK travel audiences with B1 and B2. Retarget 50% viewers of B1 with V4 and statics R3–R5. Brand search (already in the plan) catches the demand this creates.
- **Media:**
  - Google: B1 on YouTube in-stream and Demand Gen, B2 as bumpers. This replaces part of the existing 10% Demand Gen prospecting line.
  - Meta: B1 cut-downs and B2 in Reels and Stories, within prospecting, alongside the statics.
- **Flighting (UK):** pulse 2–4 weeks before each departure peak:
  - Late-October half-term (Florida, Turkey)
  - December (New York at Christmas, Dubai winter sun)
  - January to February peak booking season for summer (Turkey)
  - Easter
  - July to August departures
- **Measure:**
  - Branded search volume for "calafly" week on week
  - YouTube view rate and 30s completion; Meta ThruPlay cost
  - How often video viewers go on to visit the site
  - Purchase rate of retargeted viewers vs non-viewers
  - A Google or Meta brand-lift study once spend qualifies
