Structure

Every country page gets its own four ad groups, split by what people type. The campaign shares one budget, and spend is steered between countries ad group by ad group (see Budget).

      Brand campaign (CF_UK_SEARCH_BRAND) uses the same settings with a £0.30 click cap. It stays separate because Google reads "calafly" as a misspelling of a larger competitor. Brand terms are added as negatives in the destinations campaign so the two campaigns don't bid against each other.

      05

      Bidding, in three stages

      Plans sell for roughly £3–£18, so a 70p click can wipe out a sale. Each stage starts only when the data supports it.

      Days 1–14Maximise clicks, £0.60 cap

      Buys clean data at a controlled price. Purchases are still tracked from day one.

      After ~15 purchasesMaximise conversions

      No target yet. Google learns which searches buy.

      After 30 in 30 daysTarget CPA, then target ROAS

      Set target CPA at the actual 14-day CPA, then lower it 10% every two weeks. Switch to target ROAS once order values track reliably.

      Never change budget or bids by more than 20% at a time, or more often than every 3–4 days.

        09

        Import with Google Ads Editor

        About 15 minutes. The files are in google-search/editor/ on the TNT branch.

        1. Open Google Ads Editor, sign in, and download the CalaFly account.
        2. Shared library → Negative keyword lists: create CF_UK_NEGATIVES and paste file 06.
        3. Account → Import → From file, in this order: 01 → 02 → 03 → 04 → 05 → 07 → 08 → 09. Review and accept each one.
        4. Add the images from images/ to each ad group (file 10 lists which go where).
        5. Set the campaign goal (CF Purchase only) and observation audiences, and turn AI Max and auto-created assets off.
        6. Enter the daily budgets, then Post. Both campaigns arrive Paused.
        10

        First eight weeks

        Week 0

        Account settings, conversion and Consent Mode QA, sitelink URL check, import, then enable.

        Wk 1–2

        Leave bids alone. Review search terms twice a week. Track impression share on "turkey esim", "dubai esim" and "usa esim" exact.

        Wk 2

        Move to Maximise conversions at about 15 purchases. Shift weight between countries if CPA differs by more than 30%.

        Wk 3–4

        Add a second RSA per ad group from the best headlines in the asset report. Pause keywords that spent twice the target CPA with no purchase. Add price assets.

        Wk 5–8

        Target CPA. Raise budget 20% every 3–4 days while under target. Test AI Max in one country. Launch Standard Shopping from the Merchant Center feed.

        KPIs: cost per purchase (primary), ROAS, conversion rate by ad group, impression share on core exact terms (above 60%), CTR (above 8% non-brand, above 25% brand), and install and refund rates from CalaFly.

        Before it goes live

        1. Check the page addresses. Sitelinks assume /how-it-works, /refund-policy, /compatible-devices, /faq and /global, and ads assume /usa, /turkey and /dubai. Ads pointing to a missing page get rejected.
        2. Budget is set: £30/day destinations and £3/day brand (£1,000 for the month), already in the import files.
        3. Run one real test purchase and confirm CF Purchase records it before enabling.
