E

Optimisation routine

Sized for £1,000 a month. Small budgets need patience: decisions are made on spend thresholds, not on a day or two of data.

Launch day+2 hours
  • Campaigns → Ads: every ad shows Eligible or Approved. Fix anything "Disapproved" (usually a landing page or a sitelink URL).
  • Keywords: none show "Low search volume" across a whole ad group. If one does, that's fine as long as others in the group serve.
Days 1–75 min a day
  • Spend pacing: each campaign near its daily budget. Under 50% spent means the £0.60 cap is too low; raise that campaign's cap to £0.70.
  • Don't change anything else for the first 72 hours.
Monday + Thursdayweeks 1–4, 15 min
  • Insights and reports → Search terms for each campaign. Tick irrelevant terms → Add as negative keyword → to list CF_UK_NEGATIVES.
  • Terms that got a purchase and aren't keywords yet: add them as [exact] to the matching ad group.
  • Any term mentioning another destination: add it as a negative to this campaign only.
Every Thursday30 min
  • Cost per purchase by campaign. If one country's cost per purchase is under half another's for two weeks, move £2/day from the expensive one to the cheap one. Keep the total at £33/day.
  • Ad groups: pause an ad group that has spent £40 with no purchase while others in the same campaign are converting. Cities is the likeliest.
  • Keywords: pause a keyword that has spent £15 with no purchase and a CTR under 3%.
  • Ads → Assets: after 2,000 impressions, replace headlines rated "Low" with a new line built from the best performer.
  • Impression share: if "lost IS (budget)" is high and cost per purchase is good, that campaign is the one to give more money.
Milestones
  • 15 purchases in 30 days in a campaign: switch its bidding to Maximise conversions (Settings → Bidding → Change bid strategy).
  • 30 purchases in 30 days: add a target cost per purchase equal to its actual 14-day figure.
  • Brand searches appear (search terms containing "calafly" or "cala"): add a small brand campaign at £3/day.
  • Scaling: only when cost per purchase is below the average order value. Then +20% every 3–4 days.
End of month 1
  • Review with CalaFly: purchases, cost per purchase, install rate and refund rate by country. Decide month 2 budget.
  • Month 2 tests: AI Max on the best campaign, Shopping from Merchant Center, a second ad per ad group.
